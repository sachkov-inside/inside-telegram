Re-pitch the last message in the user's language. Start with enough context to show what is being solved and why it matters. Use ordinary words and short, natural sentences; do not compress the explanation into fragments. Keep code, commands, exact product or API names, and the established language from `CONTEXT.md` unchanged. Explain any other specialist term in plain words when it first appears. Follow `CONTEXT-MAP.md` to the relevant context when the repository has more than one.
